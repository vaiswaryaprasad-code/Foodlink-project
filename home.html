About FoodLink

Technology with a
human purpose.

FoodLink is an AI-based smart food redistribution platform designed to connect food donors with NGOs. By combining food safety analysis, expiry monitoring, donor reliability assessment, intelligent NGO recommendations and donation tracking, FoodLink helps make food redistribution safer and more efficient.
